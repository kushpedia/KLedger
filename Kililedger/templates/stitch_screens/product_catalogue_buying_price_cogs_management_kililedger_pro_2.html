How KiliLedger Landed COGS Architecture Works

When a new weekly settlement spreadsheet is parsed, KiliLedger auto-detects previously unseen Goods IDs and safely provisions new catalogue entries. Entering landed buying prices immediately unlocks authentic unit-level net pocket profit calculations across order reconciliation and margin dashboards—while keeping finalized historical statements safely locked and unmutated.
