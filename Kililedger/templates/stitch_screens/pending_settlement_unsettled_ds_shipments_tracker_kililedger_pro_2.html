Standard Operating Procedure

How Kilimall Cross-Cycle Drop-Shipping Escrow Works

Understanding why courier fees are charged before customer payment appears in your weekly settlement statements.

Read Seller Logistics Manual open_in_new
Stage 01 Day 0 (Hub Ingestion)
local_shipping DS Courier Debit

The instant your merchant parcel is barcode-scanned at the local Kilimall Drop-Shipping Hub, the fixed freight fee (e.g. KES 80 – KES 250) is deducted in that active week's statement (bill (55)).

hourglass_top Ledger status: In Transit Escrow
Stage 02 Days 2–10 (Transit & Delivery)
mark_chat_read Buyer Delivery & Inspection

Parcel travels via Kilimall Express rider to end-buyer anywhere in Kenya. Payout cannot be released while in buyer transit window or open return cooling-off duration (7-day window).

sync KiliLedger tracks aging automatically
Stage 03 Cycle +1 / +2 Statement
account_balance_wallet Net Realized Payout

Upon order acceptance, Kilimall credits gross customer remittance minus commission in bill (56) or bill (57). KiliLedger links the historical courier debit to calculate true landed margin.
