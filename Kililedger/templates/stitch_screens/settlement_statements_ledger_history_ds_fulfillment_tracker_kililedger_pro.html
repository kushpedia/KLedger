How KiliLedger Tracks Drop-Shipping Parcels Across Billing Cycles

Preventing phantom courier deductions and ensuring total reconciliation accuracy across Kilimall's staggered settlement schedules.

STAGE 01 qr_code_scanner

Upfront DS Fee Ingestion

When a package is dispatched and receives its initial sortation scan at Kilimall's Nairobi DS Hub, the courier shipping fee is deducted immediately on that week's statement workbook.

remove_circle Fee debited before delivery
STAGE 02 hourglass_top

Transit Escrow Monitoring

KiliLedger isolates the package into an active transit escrow state. The parcel is continuously cross-referenced against order tracking logs until definitive buyer delivery or return arrival.

schedule Cross-cycle holding pool
STAGE 03 verified

Cash Realization & Auto-Settlement

When Kilimall credits customer payment in subsequent statement sheets (often Cycle +1 or +2), KiliLedger links the 15-digit Order SN automatically without double-counting fees or landed costs.
